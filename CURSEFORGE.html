Profession Sage

Know what every craft earns, and the best way to your next skill level, without leaving the profession window.

Profession Sage adds profit numbers, a leveling guide and a gathering guide straight into Blizzard's own profession window. No extra window to manage: open your profession and it's there.

Profit on every recipe

  • Every recipe shows what you'd make or lose crafting it, in green or red.
  • Uses the better of the auction price (after the AH cut) or the vendor price, minus the cheapest way to buy the materials.
  • Hover a recipe for the full breakdown and the skill levels where it turns yellow, green and grey.

Leveling guide

Click the book button in the profession window's title bar, next to the close button. Type or slide the skill you want to reach and get a step-by-step route from where you are now:

  • What to craft and how many times, including crafts that won't give a point. Each step shows its skill-up chance.
  • When to learn new recipes, and where: trainers by name and zone, vendors, drops, world drops and quest rewards.
  • When to train Journeyman, Expert and Artisan, and which trainers can teach you.
  • Three route styles: Most profit, Fewest crafts or Balanced. Hover the selector to compare all three.
  • Counts what you'll make back by selling what you craft, so you see the real cost (or profit) of leveling.
  • Shopping list of everything the route needs, minus what's already in your bags and bank. Send it to Auctionator in one click.

Plan

Choose how many of a recipe you want, even more than you have materials for. The status tells you whether everything is in your bags, some is in your bank or on your other characters, or how many items you're short. If you're short, an optional small popup at the side of your screen lists what you still need and ticks items off as you loot or buy them. It stays up at the auction house and after a reload.

Gathering guide

Open Herbalism, Mining, Skinning or Fishing to see where to go at your skill, for your faction, and what you'll find there, with the most valuable first. With GatherMate2 installed, zones are sorted by the nodes you've actually found. Mining can switch to a smelting route.

Pick a target skill and the gathering guide lays out each skill range on the way, with a Train step wherever you need the next rank. Herbs, ore veins and skinnable creatures show the skill they need right in their tooltip, next to yours.

Works with (all optional)

  • Auctionator: auction prices and shopping lists. Recommended.
  • Questie: vendors, drops, quests and trainers for each recipe.
  • GatherMate2: sorts gathering zones by the nodes you've found.
  • Syndicator (or Baganator): shows which of your other characters have the materials you need.

Without them, Profession Sage uses vendor prices and remembers trainers, vendors and prices as you visit them.

Commands

  • /sage: open or close the guide (with a profession open)
  • /sage profit: show or hide profit next to recipes
  • /sage cut <percent>: the auction house cut used for profit (default 5)
  • /sage track / /sage clear: show or stop the materials popup
  • /sage errors: list errors the addon caught

Good to know

  • Recipe data comes from the WoW Forever game client itself: every recipe, its materials and its skill-up colors.
  • The skill needed to learn a trainer recipe isn't in the game data. Many are checked for WoW Forever already; the rest are estimated until you visit that profession's trainer once. After that it's exact.
  • Prices are only as fresh as your last auction house scan.
  • The gathering guide follows the classic leveling zones.
